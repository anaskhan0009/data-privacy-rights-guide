# Data Privacy Rights Guide — Know Your Rights & Generate Request Letters

Privacy laws give you enforceable rights over your own data — most people just never use them. This guide explains the **six core rights** (access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection) in plain language, then turns them into action: a **request letter generator** that produces a polite, firm, ready-to-send letter, and a **tracker** to log every request, its deadline and its outcome.

**Live Demo:** https://anaskhan0009.github.io/data-privacy-rights-guide/

## Features

- **Local account system** — sign up / log in on this device (passwords stored only as salted SHA-256 hashes, never plain text), continue as guest, per-account data separation, account deletion, and Export / Import of your records as JSON
- **Full record manager (CRUD)** — create, view, edit and delete your own saved records; the core tool works fully without an account, signing in unlocks saving — a request tracker recording company, request type, date sent and status, so nothing disappears into an inbox
- **Plain-language rights guide** — each right explained with its legal basis (GDPR articles) and its honest limits and exceptions
- **Letter generator** — name, company and request type become a complete letter citing the right, requesting third-party notification and referencing the one-month response clock
- **Sending playbook** — finding the privacy contact, identity checks, follow-ups and escalating to a data protection authority

## How to Use

1. Read the six rights and pick the one that fits your situation.
2. Generate the letter, copy it, and email it to the company’s privacy contact from your account email.
3. Log the request in the tracker below with the date sent, and update its status as replies arrive.
4. No response in a month? Follow up, then escalate to your data protection authority.

## Why These Rights Are Worth Using

Every access request teaches you what a company really keeps; every deletion request shrinks the pile that can leak in the next breach; every objection stops a marketing machine that assumes silence means consent. Companies respond to these letters every day — the system works when people use it. This page is general education, not legal advice; for a serious dispute, a local privacy lawyer or your data protection authority can help.

## Ethical Use

This is a **defensive, educational tool** — it only ever helps you understand and improve your own security and privacy. Nothing here can be used to attack, track or deceive anyone else.

## Tech

Single self-contained `index.html` — HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, WebCrypto (salted SHA-256 password hashing), localStorage persistence. No servers, no tracking, no external requests.
